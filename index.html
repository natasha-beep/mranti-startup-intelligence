How this complements MRANTI

MYStartup can answer “what does Malaysia's startup ecosystem look like?” — active startups, geographic distribution, technology/industry mix, talent and investment signals. DOSM can answer “what is happening in the market around them?” — sector revenue, e-commerce activity and household purchasing power. MRANTI internal data then answers “what did our own portfolio commercialise?”.

Why this is useful for commercialisation

Household income is not startup income. It is a market-demand indicator. It can help contextualise consumer purchasing power, affordability and geographic market prioritisation when evaluating commercial pathways for B2C or consumer-adjacent solutions.
